# Atelier v1.4.2 — 2026-09-26

One small fix to the tab strip.

## What changed

**A short tab fills its folder.** A worktree folder is always wide enough
to show its whole name. When a session's title was shorter than that name,
the tab kept its own width and left an empty gap before the folder's `+`.
The folder's tabs now share the extra width, so the last one reaches the
`+`.
