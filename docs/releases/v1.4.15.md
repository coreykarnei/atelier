# Atelier v1.4.15 — 2026-10-12

A session waiting on its background agents stays blue.

## What changed

**Done means done.** When Claude starts background agents or a workflow
and ends its turn to wait for them ("Waiting for 1 background agent to
finish"), the session used to turn green and chime as if it had
finished. Now it stays blue, with no sound or banner. When the agents
finish, Claude picks the work back up on its own, and the dot turns
green and rings only when that turn ends. Stopping the agents yourself
in Claude ends the wait the same way.

Hover the dot to see what it's waiting on: the task's name, or
"waiting on 2 agents".

Background shells don't count. A dev server or a watcher may never
finish, and it shouldn't keep a session looking busy.

Remote sessions get this too; hosts update their helper on the next
connection.
