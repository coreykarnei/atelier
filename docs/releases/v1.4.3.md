# Atelier v1.4.3 — 2026-09-26

Atelier now brings its own Claude Code hooks, and plays each sound once.

## What changed

**No more editing Claude's settings.** Atelier hands its hooks to every
Claude it starts, so there is nothing to merge into
`~/.claude/settings.json`. A `claude` started outside Atelier fires none of
them.

**Upgrading:** remove the `atelier-notify` entries you merged into
`~/.claude/settings.json` before. If they point at a different copy of the
app, every event arrives twice.

**Tabs follow `/resume` and `/clear` right away.** Switching conversations
inside Claude used to leave the tab on the old title until you sent a
message. It now changes as soon as you switch.

**Settings → Sounds.** Blow when a turn finishes, Tink when Claude needs you,
and terminal bells in both panes, all behind one switch. If your own Claude
settings already play a sound when a turn ends, Atelier leaves those two out
so you never hear two, and Settings tells you. To keep a sound hook for plain
terminals only, have it skip when `ATELIER_TAB` is set:

    [ -n "$ATELIER_TAB" ] || afplay /System/Library/Sounds/Blow.aiff

Remote hosts pick up the new hook on their next connection.
