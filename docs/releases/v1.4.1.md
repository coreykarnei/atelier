# Atelier v1.4.1 — 2026-09-25

Two small fixes to how things are labelled.

## What changed

**A new session's tab no longer flashes your whole message.** Claude
records your first message the moment you send it and a short summary a
few seconds later. The tab used to show the message in between, stretching
wide and then snapping back. Now it keeps the folder's name until the
summary arrives and changes once. A restored conversation that never got a
summary still shows its first message straight away.

**The Landing's green "recent" dot is gone.** A dot anywhere in Atelier now
means one thing: a session and its state. Recent places still come right
after shelved ones in the list.
