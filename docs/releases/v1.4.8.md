# Atelier v1.4.8 — 2026-10-07

Every session's dot now tells the truth from the moment Claude starts,
through a quit, and back.

## What changed

**New sessions show a dot right away.** A session you just started, or
one you just `/clear`ed, wears a grey dot: Claude is running, and its
conversation is empty. Before, it had no dot until you sent a message.
Your first prompt turns it blue.

**Reopened sessions show a dot right away too.** A session that comes
back from a relaunch or a `/resume` is green as soon as Claude reports
in, instead of dotless until your next message.

**A turn cut short comes back hollow.** Quitting Atelier stops the Claude
sessions running on this Mac. A session that was mid-turn used to come
back green, as if it had finished. It now comes back as a hollow blue
dot (hover: "cut off"), and stays that way until you prompt it again.
Atelier reads the conversation itself to tell, so this also works after
a crash, and a session whose turn really did finish comes back green
even if Atelier missed the moment it ended. Remote sessions keep running
on their host while Atelier is closed, so they aren't affected.

**Rings keep ringing until you look.** A turn that finished while you
were elsewhere rings green. About a minute later the ring used to stop
on its own, with a second chime and banner, as if you'd seen it. Now it
rings until you look, and there's one chime per turn.

**Working dots breathe.** The blue dot slowly fades to half brightness
and back while Claude works. It always pulsed, but too faintly to see.

**Each dot keeps its own rhythm.** Breathing and ringing dots start
their cycle when their session changes state, so a row of them no
longer moves in lockstep.

Remote hosts pick up the updated hook helper on their next connection.
