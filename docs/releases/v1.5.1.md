# Atelier v1.5.1 — 2026-10-23

Atelier comes forward at once again.

## What changed

**Switching to Atelier is instant.** Clicking Atelier in the Dock, or
switching to it any other way, could take half a second to two seconds
before it took the keyboard, and the wait grew the longer the app had
been open. The file tree was the cause. Each time it refreshed after
files changed, it left behind the folders' expand arrows from before,
and every one of those had to be redrawn whenever the window gained or
lost focus. After a day of agents editing files, that was about 30,000
of them, using about 70 MB. Now the tree reuses its arrows, so it only
ever has as many as it has shown at once.
