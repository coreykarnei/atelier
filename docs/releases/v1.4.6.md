# Atelier v1.4.6 — 2026-10-01

Open folders in the file tree pin to the top as you scroll.

## What changed

**Pinned folders sit at the top.** Scroll down inside an open folder and
its name stays pinned above the files, with any open subfolders stacked
beneath it, so you always know where the rows you're looking at live.
Since v1.0.0 that stack had been drawing at the bottom of the tree
instead.

**They slide in and out.** A folder's row now stops at the top the
moment it gets there, rather than popping in once the folder had
scrolled away. When you scroll past a folder's last file, its pinned
row is pushed up and out by it, and a subfolder slides away under its
parent. Clicking a pinned folder scrolls back to it.
