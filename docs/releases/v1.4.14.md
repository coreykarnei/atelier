# Atelier v1.4.14 — 2026-10-12

Attention dots are bigger, and the ring on an unseen one is hard to miss.

## What changed

**Bigger dots.** Session dots in the bottom bar are 8pt (were 6), and
the dots on project tabs are 9pt (were 7), with a little more room
between them. Blue and green are now easy to tell apart at a glance,
without waiting to see whether the dot breathes.

**A stronger ring.** When a session finishes or gets blocked while
you're looking elsewhere, its dot gives off a ring until you look. The
ring is now thicker, leaves the dot at full strength, and stays bright
until it's well clear of it. The old one was brightest while still
against the dot and faded fast, so a quick glance missed it about half
the time. It keeps the same 2-second beat, and it still never gets
louder the longer it waits.
