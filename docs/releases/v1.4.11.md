# Atelier v1.4.11 — 2026-10-10

The selected project is easier to spot, and worktree folders sit
closer together.

## What changed

**The selected project stands out.** The selected tab along the top
is now a lighter, raised tab with rounded corners, and the other
projects sit as darker, square tabs. The two used to differ by a single
shade, and with the window's opacity turned down they looked the same.
The new-project `+` at the end of the row is a little brighter too.

**Worktree folders, tidied.** The label tab above a worktree's sessions
now slants out on its right side, like a folder's tab, and neighbouring
folders sit closer together.
